## 1. Rooftop Solar Generation & Carbon Abatement in India

- **Average Solar Irradiance Yield**: In Indian climatic conditions, a grid-connected rooftop solar PV system generates **4.0 to 4.5 kWh (units) per kW per day** across the year (conservative planning benchmark: **`4.2 kWh/kW/day`** = **`126 kWh/month per kW`**).
- **Grid Emission Displacement**: Using the official CEA FY 2025-26 grid weighted average (`0.675 kg CO₂/kWh`):
  - **1 kW Rooftop Solar**: Generates ~`126 units/month` (`1,533 units/year`) → avoids **`85.05 kg CO₂/month`** (**`1,034.8 kg CO₂/year`**, ~`1.03 tonnes/year`).
  - **2 kW Rooftop Solar**: Generates ~`252 units/month` → avoids **`170.10 kg CO₂/month`** (**`2.07 tonnes CO₂/year`**).
  - **3 kW Rooftop Solar** *(flagship household size under PM Surya Ghar)*: Generates ~`378 units/month` → avoids **`255.15 kg CO₂/month`** (**`3.10 tonnes CO₂/year`**).
- **Financial Savings Benchmark**: Assuming typical Indian residential tariffs of **₹6.50 to ₹8.50 per unit (kWh)** (average `₹7.50/unit`), a 3 kW system saves roughly **₹2,835/month** (~₹34,000/year) on electricity bills, with central subsidies under PM Surya Ghar covering up to ₹78,000 for 3 kW+ residential systems.
- **National Mission Target**: 1 crore residential households, yielding **1,000 billion units** of clean electricity and **720 million tonnes CO₂e** abated over 25 years.

## 2. Household & Hostel Appliance Benchmarks (at `0.675 kg CO₂/kWh`)

| Appliance | Typical Power Rating | Energy per Hour / Use | Carbon Footprint (`kg CO₂`, CEA 2026 Grid) | Practical Saving Tip |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Split AC (1.5 Ton)** | `1,500 W` | `1.50 kWh / hr` | **1.0125 kg / hr** | Setting AC to `24°C–26°C` instead of `20°C` saves ~24% power (`0.243 kg CO₂/hr` saved). |
| **Electric Geyser / Immersion Rod** | `2,000 W` | `0.50 kWh / 15-min bath` | **0.3375 kg / use** | Shortening hot-water heating by 5 min saves `~0.11 kg CO₂` per bath. |
| **Desktop PC / Gaming Rig** | `200 W` | `0.20 kWh / hr` | **0.1350 kg / hr** | Sleep mode cuts idle draw by 90%. |
| **Desert Air Cooler** | `200 W` | `0.20 kWh / hr` | **0.1350 kg / hr** | Uses ~86% less energy than a Split AC. |
| **Electric Kettle** | `1,500 W` | `0.10 kWh / boil` | **0.0675 kg / use** | Boil only the cups needed. |
| **Standard Ceiling Fan** | `75 W` | `0.075 kWh / hr` | **0.0506 kg / hr** | Upgrading to a BEE 5-Star BLDC Fan (`28 W`) cuts this to **`0.0189 kg/hr`** (`-63%`). |
| **Laptop (Active)** | `65 W` | `0.065 kWh / hr` | **0.0439 kg / hr** | Power-saver mode saves ~25%. |
| **LED Study Lamp** | `10 W` | `0.010 kWh / hr` | **0.00675 kg / hr** | 85% lower than incandescent bulbs. |
