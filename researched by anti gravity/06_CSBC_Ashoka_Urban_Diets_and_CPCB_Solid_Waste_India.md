## 1. Embodied Carbon of Indian Diets

According to **Pathak et al. (2010)** (peer-reviewed Indian dietary LCA) and **CSBC Ashoka University (2024)**:

| Diet Pattern | Daily Footprint (`kg CO₂e/person/day`) | Per Meal (~3 meals/day) | Notes & Sources |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pure Vegetarian (Local Plant-Based)** | **0.724** | **0.241** | Lowest footprint; pulses, millets, seasonal vegetables (Pathak et al., 2010). |
| **Lacto-Vegetarian (High Dairy)** | **1.013** | **0.338** | `1.4×` base vegetarian; dairy alone can reach up to `3.21 kg CO₂e/CU/day` in high-income urban diets (Athare et al., 2022). |
| **Non-Veg (Chicken / Fish)** | **1.086** | **0.362** | `1.5×` base vegetarian (Pathak et al., 2010). |
| **Non-Veg (Mutton / Red Meat)** | **1.303** | **0.434** | `1.8×` base vegetarian (Pathak et al., 2010). |

### Five Low-Carbon Food Behaviours Identified by CSBC (Delhi & Bengaluru Fieldwork, 2024)
1. **Buying locally sourced food** (reduces cold-chain & food-mile transport emissions).
2. **Adopting plant-based meals** (cuts dairy/meat livestock methane).
3. **Opting for seasonal produce** (avoids energy-intensive off-season cold storage).
4. **Choosing fresh home-cooked food over ultra-processed/packaged food** (eliminates multi-layer plastic packaging & processing energy).
5. **Practising efficient food consumption** — **India wastes `50 kg` of household food per capita per year (`68.76 million tonnes/year`, UNEP 2021)**. Avoiding `0.14 kg/day` of cooked food waste prevents ~`0.35 kg CO₂e/day`.

## 2. Municipal Solid Waste (MSW) Disposal Pathways in India

Aligned with **CPCB**, **GHG Platform India**, and **IPCC Tier 1/Tier 2 First-Order Decay (FOD)** parameters for Indian MSW (~`50–52%` degradable organic/wet fraction):

| Waste Pathway | Emission Factor (`kg CO₂e/kg waste`) | Why It Matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Unsegregated Mixed Waste (Open Dump / Unmanaged Landfill)** | **0.58 – 0.63** *(CarbCalc baseline: **0.63**)* | Wet food scraps buried without oxygen rot into methane ($\text{CH}_4$, 28× more potent than $\text{CO}_2$ over 100 years). |
| **Sanitary / Controlled Landfill (Partial Gas Capture)** | **0.20 – 0.35** | Captures partial landfill gas, still higher than aerobic treatment. |
| **Source-Segregated & Composted (Wet) + Recycled (Dry)** | **0.03** *(saves **0.60 kg CO₂e/kg**)* | Aerobic composting prevents anaerobic methane formation; dry recycling displaces virgin plastic/paper production. |
